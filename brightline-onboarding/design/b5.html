API reference › Authorizations › Response fields

amount_authorized

The amount the issuer approved, in minor units. It equals amount on a full approval and is lower on a partial approval. Your host must settle for this value, never for the amount asked.

Your three misses returned the amount asked. The network expects the approved amount: amount_authorized on the response, not amount on the request.

Walkthrough · handle a partial approval

  1. Read decision. On partial, take amount_authorized as the approved amount.
  2. Map it to ISO 8583 field 4 on the response to your terminal, and keep the asked amount in field 95 so the terminal can prompt for the balance.
  3. Settle for amount_authorized. The clearing record must not exceed it; the network rejects the line otherwise.
// after the response lands
const approved =
  auth.decision === "partial"
    ? auth.amountAuthorized  // 4000
    : auth.amount;

iso.set(4, approved);      // DE4
iso.set(95, auth.amount);  // DE95
