Brightline assistant prototypes

An assistant inside the network's portals

Two concept prototypes for Brightline Bank, a fictional issuer joining a card network: one guides an engineer through technical onboarding turn by turn, the other builds the assistant into each portal's home.

Brightline Onboarding

Priya, the lead integration engineer, describes what she is building; the assistant draws her route, starts the long-lead requests other people hold, and prepares every step for her approval, with a step rail and reference pages beside the conversation.

Brightline Assist

The three portals after onboarding (Dana in the Partner Portal, Marcus in the Agent Portal, Priya in the Developer Portal), each with a briefing home and an assistant that answers, drills down and prepares the action.
